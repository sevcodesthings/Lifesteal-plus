# 💖 | What is Lifesteal+?
Lifesteal+ is a fan-made custom plugin I'm working on as an extension to Lifesteal SMP's
plugin, this is completely fan made and is NOT associated with Lifesteal SMP or any of its members.
## ❔| Can I use this?
Yes, you can use this plugin however you want. Whether it be for having a cool, free plugin to play
with your friends, or for learning Skript yourself. I don't support using this to claim as your own,
however I will not be making a license either way, as I don't care.

## ⁕ | Commands
### /withdraw <integer> 
Allows you to withdraw any amount of hearts that you have (you cannot withdraw yourself into 0 hearts)
### /sethearts <player> <number> (OP command)
Lets operators set the heart count of any player
### /resethearts <player> <number> (OP command)
Allows operators to reset the heart count of any player back up to exactly 10
### /lsgive <player> <string> (OP command)
Allows operators to give themselves, or any other players, any of the Lifesteal+ items (includes tab completions)

## ※ | Recipes
There is only one recipe in this plugin, which is for the Heart Essence
Recipe: heart, heart, heart, heart, heavy core, heart, heart, heart, heart

heavy = heavy core:
[heart] [heart] [heart]
[heart] [heavy] [heart]
[heart] [heart] [heart]

## ◈ | Item functionality and how to obtain them
When right-clicking a Heart Essence, you get to choose between 3 different items:
Lifesteal Sword, Heart Shield, and the Heart Chestplate

### Lifesteal Sword:
Gives you 2x the amount of hearts when killing players (2 instead of 1)
### Heart Shield:
Gives all nearby players darkness 1 and stops them from being able to consume anything
for 10 seconds when right-clicked, has a cooldown of 60 seconds
### Heart Chestplate:
No abilities in particular, just has protection 5 (V)
